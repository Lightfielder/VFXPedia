# About

The vfxpedia domain is owned and operated by eyeon Software, and provided free of charge as a free public repository of information related to compositing and visual effects in general, and software produced by eyeon Software, specifically. While eyeon Software owns and operates the servers containing vfxpedia.com, all content provided on these pages is licensed as described in copyrights.

Much of the information posted to this site was initially written and added by employees of eyeon Software, and the content is often reviewed and updated by our staff. However, the goal of this site is to provide a wide base of information useful to the visual effects industry as a whole. As a result, accounts with editorial access to the content on this site are available to all.
