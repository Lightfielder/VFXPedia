## Online Forums

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Pigsfly_thumb.jpg)

[**Pigsfly DF User Group***(www.pigsfly.com)*](http://www.pigsfly.com/forums/index.php)

type: online forum

Pigsfly is the most active and largest user forum addressing Fusion concerns and questions. Anything and everything related to post-production and visual effects can be addressed on Pigsfly. This forum is a useful aid for experienced compositors or a new user looking to get a better understanding of who is using Fusion and what they are doing with it! This list is constantly monitored by eyeon Software Product Specialists and Developers.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Laffey_thumb.jpg)

[**Fusion-L Mailing List***(fusion.laffeycomputer.com)*](http://fusion.laffeycomputer.com/)

type: email subscription

Fusion-L is an email list dedicated specifically to Fusion and DFX+ compositing software. It was originally setup to provide an email subscription-based option to those users who prefer this format. This email list is monitored on a daily basis by eyeon Software Product Specialists and Developers in addition to hundreds of other users.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Fxguide_thumb.jpg)

[**FXGuide***(www.fxguide.com)*](http://www.fxguide.com/)

type: online news blog

FXGuide has long been one of the most trusted sites when it comes to news and rumors concerning the high-end compositing industry. FXGuide recently launched a new section of online forums with a dedicated Fusion discussion that is monitored on a daily basis by eyeon Software Product Specialists.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Cgtalk_thumb.jpg)

[**CG Talk***(www.cgtalk.com)*](http://www.cgtalk.com/)

type: online forum

CG Talk is an online forum dealing with a wide variety of CG, post production and visual effects-related issues, with a large registered base of users. There are several sections dedicated to specific CG-related applications, including one for Fusion that is monitored on a daily basis by eyeon Software Product Specialists. This is also a great resource for keeping up on what is new in the CG and post-production industry.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Cgchannel_thumb.jpg)

[**CG Channel***(www.cgchannel.com)*](http://www.cgchannel.com/forum/index)

type: online forum

CG Channel is an excellent source for industry news and events, and has recently added a forum section with a Fusion discussion. This forum is monitored on a daily basis by eyeon Product Specialists.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Vfxtalk_thumb.jpg)

[**VFXTalk***(www.vfxtalk.com)*](http://www.vfxtalk.com/forum/)

type: online forum

VFXTalk is a recently launched visual effects forum looking to become one of the best online forums for learning digital compositing and visual effects techniques. VFXTalk currently has forums for all of the major software and hardware based compositing packages, including one for Fusion. This forum is monitored on a daily basis by eyeon Product Specialists.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Vfxsoup_thumb.jpg)

[**vfxsoup***(www.vfxsoup.com)*](http://www.vfxsoup.com/)

type: online forum

This website has been created for all those interested in Visual Effects Supervision or co-ordination. The idea is quite simple... pull together all the ideas, theories, experiences and techniques into one area for all to use and learn from. The idea is to keep it free, open and beneficial to all who participate... This online forum is monitored on a daily basis by eyeon Software Product Specialists in addition to hundreds of	other users.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Newtek_thumb.jpg)

[**NewTek Fusion Forum***(www.newtek.com)*](http://vbulletin.newtek.com/)

type: online forum

The NewTek forums are a new resource for Fusion and DFX+ users.  These forums not only address the integration between NewTek products, including Toaster 2, LightWave and Fusion / DFX+, but also provide places to show off completed work or share tips and techniques for working with these products together. This list is monitored on a daily basis by eyeon Software Product Specialists.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Dpsedits_thumb.jpg)

[**DPSedits User Group***(www.yahoo.com)*](http://groups.yahoo.com/group/DPSedits/)

type: email subscription / online forum

DPS Edits is primarily a resource for those using products offered by the Leitch Post Production division (DPS). However, since every DPS product comes bundled with the base version of our DFX+ software, this is a valuable resource for anyone using these tools together to talk to other users about how they use these products. This list is constantly monitored by eyeon Software Product Specialists.

![](Eyeon%20Manual/images/Render_ru.jpg)

[**Render.ru Fusion Forum***(www.render.ru)*](http://render.ru/forum/viewforum.php?forum_id=45)

type: online forum

The biggest Russian forum about Eyeon Fusion with a lot of unique sorted tips and tricks. Contains also Fusion FAQ. All questions are answered in real time there. Though, Render.ru is the only online forum that is not constantly monitored by Eyeon Software Product Specialists and Developers :)
